Welcome to Thistle

Thistle is a neighbourhood continental restaurant for people who love good food and good company. We take European classics, from Italian pasta to French pastries and Spanish tapas, and cook them with seasonal ingredients and a modern touch.

Relaxed evenings, long brunches or a quick coffee and dessert. There is always a seat for you.
