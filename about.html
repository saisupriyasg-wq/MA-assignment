About Thistle

Rooted in tradition, grown for today.

Our Story

Thistle began as a weekend supper club run by two friends who missed the simple, honest food they had eaten while travelling through Italy, France and Spain. Their small dinners grew so popular that, in 2022, they opened a restaurant of their own.

The name comes from the thistle, a tough, beautiful flower that thrives anywhere. We hope our food feels the same: comforting, vibrant and a little unexpected.

Our Mission

To serve thoughtful continental food that brings people together, using fresh, seasonal and responsibly sourced ingredients.

Our Vision

To become the city's most loved neighbourhood table, where every guest feels like a regular.

What Makes Us Different

Pasta rolled in-house every morning, a menu that changes with the seasons, and classic recipes with a modern, Instagram-worthy finish.
